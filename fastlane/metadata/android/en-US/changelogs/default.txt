Initial Google Play release of Pin Manager for Pebble:
• Create, edit, and delete custom Pebble Timeline pins locally via PebbleKit Android 2
• Customize pin icons, durations, and accent colors
• Optional 100% on-device Notification Auto-Pins with per-app allowlist, keyword/regex rules, and Rule Tester
• Watchless "Run without a watch" mode with Simulated Timeline preview
• Adaptive phone, foldable two-pane, and landscape Material 3 layouts
• Zero internet permission, no ads, no tracking
